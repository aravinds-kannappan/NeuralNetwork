## Limits that travel with the result (SPEC §13.5)

One model and one checkpoint series, Olmo-3 7B; no replication at another scale, seed or model. The planted reference is measured at T_15 only, so birth numbers are read against a band from a later checkpoint; room with the jobs' own birth ceilings against that band is 0.195, under the frozen 0.200 line read at T_15 (`REVIEW_2026-11-01.md` D2). Attention-routed computation is not measured; drivers are MLP neurons chosen by the direct path, and the ablation removes every path of those neurons. Labels come from UNITE's clusterer (sample 0), not from a judge; with 57 related and 93 unrelated jobs R4's interval is 0.16 wide and says so. Mean replacement is an off-distribution intervention; the identity gate and the energy-matched random arm (median fall 0.007) bound its effect and do not remove it. The ceiling splits are UNITE's; 15 of the 65 setting splits are also primary ceilings, and room is reported both ways. A null on R1 is a finding in its own right and is reported as one. The job-fall rule is a mean of per-context ratios and can be decided by one small-activation context (Result 12). The relatedness label is a property of the context clusters, not of the neuron's wiring, so Result 4 is correlational across jobs even though each I is causal within a job.
